## GeoHarvest 0.1.2 beta: a new installer, and updates without the black window

GeoHarvest now has its own installer. It looks like the app and shows every step as it
works. Updating no longer opens a black command window: "Update now" shows the installer's
progress window and reopens GeoHarvest when it's done. GeoHarvest also stops offering an
update you already have. Uninstall is in Settings, and it asks whether to keep your searches,
leads and settings. No account, no sign-in: everything stays on your computer.

### Download and install

1. Download **GeoHarvest-v0.1.2-beta-windows.zip** below (Windows 10 and 11).
2. Right-click the zip and choose **Extract All**. Setup can't run from inside the zip.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click **GeoHarvest-Setup.exe**, then click **Install**.
4. At the end, choose the Start menu entry, a desktop shortcut and whether GeoHarvest opens
   when Windows starts.

**"Windows protected your PC"?** GeoHarvest is not code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.

**Coming from 0.1.1?** Install 0.1.2 by hand, once, as above. It installs over 0.1.1 and keeps
all your searches, leads, settings and AI key. From 0.1.2 on, **Update now** works without
the black window.
