## GeoHarvest 0.4.0 beta: safer, lighter, and a lot of small fixes

This update comes from a full check of the app. It adds a way to free up disk space, makes
backups and exports safer, speeds up the Leads page with big lists, and fixes many small
things that could go wrong during a search.

### Free up space

- **"Free up space" in Settings.** After many big searches GeoHarvest's data can grow to
  hundreds of megabytes. The new button shows how much can be freed and asks first. It only
  removes stored details of businesses your finished searches left out. Your leads, your
  searches and anything an unfinished search needs are kept.

### Safer backups

- **Restoring a backup no longer freezes the window.** It runs in the background and tells
  you when it is done.
- **A backup that stops half-way never looks like a real one,** and can't replace a good one.
- **Older backups work straight away** after restoring, with no restart.
- **The safety copy made before a restore** is always kept (two restores in a row no longer
  overwrite it). GeoHarvest keeps the newest five.

### Exports

- **An export that fails never leaves half a file** or spoils the file it was replacing.
- **The HomingPigeon export tells you how many leads had no email** and were left out.
- **Fewer surprises in Excel and Google Sheets:** empty cells stay empty when pasted, and
  text that a spreadsheet could read as a formula is kept as plain text.

### Smart mode

- **Smart mode stops and says why** when the AI keeps refusing (no balance left, a wrong
  model or web address), instead of failing again and again.
- **A lead the AI could not score is sent once per search run,** so it no longer uses up your
  AI limit.

### Faster with big lists

- **The Leads page does much less work** with tens of thousands of leads: exporting, copying
  and filling in a saved search are quicker.

### Fixed

- **A search can no longer get stuck as "running"** after an error. It stops, and the error
  is written to the crash log.
- **The search screens keep up:** pressing Run again while a search runs, buttons that stayed
  greyed out, and a deleted search still showing its leads are all fixed.
- **Websites checked while the internet is down** are visited again later, instead of the
  leads being dropped as "no email found".
- **Emails written with capital letters** (Info@Shop.Com) are saved whole.
- **A company's age is looked up again** when the domain registry is busy, instead of being
  marked as unknown.
- **Saved AI keys and proxies can't be wiped by a damaged file** any more.
- **Uninstall only ever empties GeoHarvest's own folder.**
