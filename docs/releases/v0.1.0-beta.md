## GeoHarvest 0.1.0 beta: the first release 🎉

GeoHarvest finds business leads on Google Maps. Tell it the kind of business and where, and
it collects every match with phone, website, address, rating and more, finds emails and
social media pages on each business's website, and exports everything to Excel. Free, fast,
and no API key needed.

### What it does

- **Any business, any country.** Tick the countries (or the whole European Union or Gulf in
  one click), then the cities. Several business types in one search.
- **Fast.** No web browser in the loop: GeoHarvest asks Google Maps for results directly and
  searches several cities at once. About 200 businesses per city and business type.
- **Maximum detail.** Phone, website, full address, coordinates, rating, reviews, opening
  hours, features, claimed status, Google Maps link and more.
- **Emails and social media** from each business's website: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn,
  X, YouTube and TikTok.
- **Startup or established.** Company age estimated from the website's registration date,
  with a filter for each.
- **Filters:** minimum rating and reviews, must have a website or phone, leave out closed
  businesses or ones you already found.
- **Smart mode (optional):** connect a free AI (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral), a paid
  one (Claude, OpenAI, DeepSeek) or one on your own PC (Ollama, LM Studio). It scores every
  lead from 0 to 100 against your ideal customer, with a one-line reason.
- **Excel and CSV export**, **resume** any stopped search, **past searches**, and
  **automatic updates** from now on.

### Download and install

1. Download **GeoHarvest-v0.1.0-beta-windows.zip** below (Windows 10 and 11).
2. Right-click it > **Extract All**.
3. Open the `GeoHarvest` folder and double-click **GeoHarvest.exe**.

**"Windows protected your PC"?** The app is not code-signed yet. Press **More info**, then
**Run anyway**. You only need to do this once.

### Good to know

- This is a **beta**: please [report anything confusing or broken](https://github.com/4bdulllahh/GeoHarvest/issues/new).
- Very large searches can make Google ask GeoHarvest to slow down. Nothing is lost: wait
  about 15 minutes and press **Resume** in Past searches.
- Company age is an estimate, and some countries' registries (such as `.pk` and `.de`) do
  not publish dates, so it is sometimes blank.
- When you contact the businesses you find, follow the rules where you and they are (GDPR,
  CAN-SPAM and others) and Google's terms of service.
