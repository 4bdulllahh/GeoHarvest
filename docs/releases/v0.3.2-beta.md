## GeoHarvest 0.3.2 beta: pause, resume and carry on by itself

Long searches now look after themselves. GeoHarvest waits out Google's limits on its own and
carries on when you reopen it, and you can pause a search and come back to it later.

### Start, pause, resume, stop

- **Pause and Resume on the New search page.** Pause a search, close GeoHarvest if you like, and
  press Resume whenever you're ready, even days later.
- **Google's "slow down" no longer stops you.** GeoHarvest shows a countdown and starts again by
  itself when the wait is over, usually after 15 minutes. If Google limits it again soon after,
  it waits longer (30, then 60 minutes). **Resume now** skips the wait.
- **Website checks and Smart mode keep going during the wait,** because they don't use Google.
- **Close the app mid-search and it carries on.** Next time you open GeoHarvest, the search picks
  up by itself, even if the wait for Google was still running. This also works after a crash or
  an update. A search you paused stays paused, and one you stopped stays stopped.
- **Resume is right there.** A stopped search can be resumed from the New search page, so you
  don't have to go to Past searches. Past searches can pause the running search too.

### Fewer "slow down" messages, and nothing searched twice

- **Requests to Google are now spaced out,** instead of being sent as fast as Google answers. This
  should make limits less likely, but big searches take longer. Gentle, Normal and Fast still set
  the pace, and the estimate on the form allows for it.
- **Resuming carries on from the exact page,** so it doesn't use up your Google allowance asking
  for the same results again.

### Fixed

- **Leads now come from all your places.** A search that reached its maximum could fill up from
  the first city and the first few business types. Now every city gets a turn before any city
  gets a second one.
- **Businesses could go missing after a resume.** If Google pushed back or you pressed Stop at
  the wrong moment, a search could be marked finished with pages still to come.
- **Scrolling inside a list stays inside the list.** When the countries, cities or business types
  list reaches its end, the page no longer starts moving.
- **Scrolling past a number box no longer changes it.** Minimum leads, for example, could change
  as the pointer passed over it. Click the box first if you do want to use the wheel.
- **Long messages under the search no longer get cut off.**

### Smaller changes

- **Click a country or city's name to tick it,** not only its switch.
- **The model drop-down in Smart mode works.** It fills by itself once there's a key, and the
  arrow opens the list. You can still type a model's name.
- **"On Friday at 20:00" is now "At a set time".** Picking a day or time switches it on.
