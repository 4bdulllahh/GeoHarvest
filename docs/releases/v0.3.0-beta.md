## GeoHarvest 0.3.0 beta: requirements, minimum leads, themes and five languages

The biggest update so far. GeoHarvest now keeps searching until it has the number of leads you
asked for, and it works for cold email, cold calling or building your own database.

### Finding leads

- **Requirements** (was "Which businesses?"): click a requirement to switch it on or off. New
  ones: must have an email, social media, LinkedIn, the owner's name, a listing claimed by the
  owner, or opening hours listed.
- **Sliders for anything with a minimum and maximum:** Google rating, number of reviews and
  company age in years. Quick buttons for Any age, Startups, Established or your own range.
- **Minimum leads** (was "Stop after"): GeoHarvest keeps searching, digging deeper into each
  city, until at least that many businesses meet your requirements. Every match found along
  the way is kept, best first. When there still aren't enough, it says why and offers to add
  more cities or related business types.
- **Quick setup:** one click sets the requirements for Cold email, Cold calling or Build a
  database. **Presets:** save your own requirements and details, and use them again in one
  click.
- **Quality score:** every lead is scored on what is known about the business (email, phone,
  website, owner, social media, address, rating, reviews...). Double-click a lead to see what
  it scored on and what is missing.
- **Name can be switched off** in Details to include. With nothing picked, Name is used.
- **Live estimate:** the form shows roughly how many businesses the search will look at and
  how long it will take.
- **A notification when a search finishes** while you are in another window.

### The Leads page

- **Sort** best leads first, by quality or AI score, name or city A to Z, rating, reviews or
  date found. **Show** only leads with an email, a phone, both, a website, no website, the
  owner's name, social media, LinkedIn, startups, established businesses, good fits, or those
  **not exported yet**.
- **Date filter** on the day each lead was found, and **rating and reviews sliders**.
- **Copy the emails or phone numbers** shown (or selected), one per line.
- **Export for HomingPigeon:** one row per email, ready to import into HomingPigeon.
- New columns: **Found on** and **Exported on**.

### Look and feel

- **Themes**, and a **theme maker** to make your own and share it as a file (themes made in
  HomingPigeon load too), **smooth scrolling**, and Reset appearance.
- **Menu order:** drag the pages into the order you want, and hide the ones you never use.
- **Resizing:** rows wrap, side-by-side parts stack, and below about 1000 pixels the sidebar
  shrinks to icons. GeoHarvest remembers its window size and place.
- **Five languages:** English, Arabic (right to left), German, Spanish and French, in Settings.
- **Start here:** a guide page that ticks off each step as you do it.

### Your data

- **Backup and restore:** keep your searches, leads, presets and settings safe, or move them to
  a new PC. GeoHarvest also backs up by itself once a week and keeps the last four.
- **Settings:** the first page when GeoHarvest opens, date and time formats, forget saved keys,
  and report a problem.

### Updates

- **Updates install by themselves:** every time GeoHarvest opens, it downloads the newest
  version and installs it, waiting until a running search finishes.
- **From now on, an old Setup installs the newest release:** a Setup from 0.3.0 or later first
  looks for a newer version and installs that one instead.

### Download and install

1. Download **GeoHarvest-v0.3.0-beta-windows.zip** below (Windows 10 and 11).
2. Right-click the zip and choose **Extract All**. Setup can't run from inside the zip.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click **GeoHarvest-Setup.exe**, then click **Install**.

**"Windows protected your PC"?** GeoHarvest is not code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.

**"Smart App Control blocked an app"?** Some Windows 11 PCs block every unsigned program, with
no Run anyway. A backup installer for those PCs is coming in 0.3.1.

**Already have GeoHarvest?** Use **Update now** in the app. Your searches, leads, settings and
AI key are kept. Your old "Stop after" and company age choices are carried over.
