## GeoHarvest 0.1.1 beta: a proper installer, exact lead counts and a Stop that stops

### New

- **A real Windows installer.** The download now contains `GeoHarvest-Setup-v0.1.1-beta.exe`.
  Double-click it and it installs GeoHarvest for you. It adds GeoHarvest to the Start menu,
  can add a desktop shortcut, and can open GeoHarvest when Windows starts. It installs for
  your Windows account only, so it doesn't need administrator rights.
- **Uninstall from inside the app.** Settings > **Uninstall GeoHarvest...** removes the app.
  You can also uninstall it from Windows Settings > Apps. Either way, it asks whether to keep
  your searches, leads and settings.
- **"Stop after" gives you exactly that many leads.** Ask for 200 and you get 200. Before,
  leads removed by the company age filter or by Smart mode's minimum score still counted, so
  you could end up with fewer. Now GeoHarvest keeps searching until 200 leads are left after
  every check, or until Google has no more to show.
- **No repeats by default.** "Leave out businesses I found in earlier searches" is now ticked
  for new users, so a 200-lead search gives you 200 businesses you haven't found before. If
  you used 0.1.0, GeoHarvest remembers your last settings, so tick the box once.

### Fixed

- **Stop now stops right away.** Before, the search kept checking websites (and waiting for
  the AI) for up to a minute after you pressed Stop. Now it stops within a second or two.
  Nothing is lost: press **Resume** in Past searches to pick up the websites it hadn't
  checked yet.

### Download and install

1. Download **GeoHarvest-v0.1.1-beta-windows.zip** below (Windows 10 and 11).
2. Open the zip and double-click **GeoHarvest-Setup-v0.1.1-beta.exe**.
3. Follow the steps. Tick **Open GeoHarvest when Windows starts** if you want that.

**"Windows protected your PC"?** The installer is not code-signed yet. Press **More info**,
then **Run anyway**.

**Coming from 0.1.0?** Install this version by hand, once. The 0.1.0 updater can't open the
new installer, so its "Update now" button shows an error. Your searches, leads, settings and
AI key are stored separately from the app, so the new version finds them. You can then delete
your old `GeoHarvest` folder. From 0.1.1 on, updates install themselves again.

### Privacy

GeoHarvest has no accounts and no sign-in. Your searches, leads, settings and encrypted AI key
stay on your computer, in `%APPDATA%\GeoHarvest`.
