## GeoHarvest 0.3.3 beta: faster, steadier searches

Big searches show their leads sooner, find more in busy cities and recover by themselves from
Google's limits and from a dropped internet connection. Updates now ask before they install.

### Leads sooner, and more of them

- **Leads appear while the search runs.** A big search used to work for a long time before
  anything new showed up. Now it works in shorter rounds, and each round's new leads go
  straight into the Leads page.
- **More businesses from busy cities.** Google lists only about 200 businesses per search.
  GeoHarvest now splits a full city into smaller map areas, and splits those again where they
  are still full, so it spends its requests where the businesses are.
- **A search keeps going to your maximum.** It no longer stops early once the minimum is reached.
- **Less waiting on big searches.** Progress is saved in a lighter way, so a long search no
  longer slows down as it grows.

### Google's limits and lost connections

- **GeoHarvest slows down by itself** when Google starts answering slowly, to avoid a limit,
  and speeds up again afterwards.
- **A limit is waited out inside the search.** GeoHarvest pauses for 2 minutes, then 5 if
  needed, and carries on. Only if Google still says no does the long wait start (15, 30, then
  60 minutes), as before.
- **No internet? The search waits.** If the connection drops, GeoHarvest tries again every 30
  seconds for up to 10 minutes and carries on when it is back.

### Warnings that save you time

- **Opening hours.** With more than one "Open" choice switched on, a business has to fit all of
  them, so most are left out. The form now says so before you start.
- **Similar business types.** Types such as "builder" and "home builder" find mostly the same
  businesses. The form names them, and the search stops asking Google for repeats.

### Updates ask first

- **"Update now" or "Later".** When a new version has downloaded, GeoHarvest asks. There is no
  countdown any more. Choose Later (or close the window) and it asks again the next time you
  open GeoHarvest.

### Fixed

- **Resume shows your real numbers at once.** The Leads, Websites and Email tiles used to start
  from zero after a resume.
- **Leads keep their history.** The date a lead was found and its "exported" mark no longer
  reset while a search carries on.
- **Deleted leads stay deleted.** A lead you removed could come back when its search resumed.
- **A waiting search no longer starts behind a question window.**
