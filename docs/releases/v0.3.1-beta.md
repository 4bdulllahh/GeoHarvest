## GeoHarvest 0.3.1 beta: smooth on older PCs, maximum leads and a smarter Smart mode

A speed and reliability update. GeoHarvest no longer freezes on older laptops, searches in
small batches, and Smart mode can write your opening lines.

### No more freezing

- **Fixed: "Not Responding" while checking websites.** Reading some websites (ones with
  pictures stored inside the page) could keep the PC busy for minutes, so Windows said
  GeoHarvest was not responding and offered to close it. Those pages are now read in a
  fraction of a second.
- **Searches run in batches of 100.** GeoHarvest finds about 100 businesses, checks their
  websites, adds the ones that fit to your leads, then finds the next 100. It never queues
  thousands of websites at once any more, so older PCs stay smooth.
- **Fairer batches:** the cities and business types take turns, so the first batch already
  mixes every city instead of filling up from the first one.
- **The Speed setting now also sets how many websites are checked at once** (Gentle: 6,
  Normal: 12, Fast: 16). On an older PC, pick Gentle in Settings.

### Maximum leads

- **A Maximum leads box next to Minimum leads.** GeoHarvest stops at the maximum and keeps the
  best ones.
- **Left on Any,** the maximum is 3 times the minimum, at most 1,500 (1,200 if both are on
  Any). You can type a maximum of up to 3,000.
- **Minimum leads** now means "dig deeper into each city until there are at least this many"
  when Google runs out first.
- The Leads tile shows **"118 of 300"** against the maximum, so it can't go over any more.

### Smart mode

- **What the AI adds:** an **opening line** for a cold email or call, **what each business
  seems to need** (for your pitch) and the **best way to reach it** (email, phone, WhatsApp,
  Instagram...). Each one is a new column you can export.
- **Hot, Warm or Cold** for every lead the AI checks, as a column and in the Show menu on the
  Leads page.
- **Never a fit:** list what you never want (chains, government offices...) and the AI scores
  those 0.
- **Saved descriptions:** save several ideal customers ("Dentists for SEO", "Clinics for a
  booking app") and switch with one click. **Fill in an example** gives you a ready one to edit.
- **Let the AI read the website:** better scores and opening lines from the start of each
  business's own website.
- **Limits:** "AI checks at most N leads per search", "at most N requests a minute" (free keys
  stop running into limit errors), and "only check leads with a phone number or an email".
- **Try it on my leads:** scores 5 of your newest leads, so you can tune your description
  before a big search.
- **The AI answers in your language:** reasons and opening lines come back in the language
  GeoHarvest is set to.

### Smaller changes

- **Phone (international) is really international now:** +971 55 123 4567 instead of
  0551234567, for every country. Leads found before this update keep the number they had.
- **Start here** is at the top of the menu. If you've already rearranged the menu, yours stays
  as it is.
