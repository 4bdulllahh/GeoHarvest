## GeoHarvest 0.1.3 beta: a lighter download, a fixed installer, and more control over your leads

Thanks to everyone who tested 0.1.2. This version fixes the installer and adds the things
you asked for.

### Fixed

- **Setup could fill your disk and fail at "Finish up".** If you extracted the zip straight
  into Downloads, Setup copied your whole Downloads folder while saving the uninstaller. Now
  it copies only its own files, and installing takes a few seconds. Running this Setup also
  deletes the extra copies the old one made, so you get that disk space back.
- **Cancel left GeoHarvest broken.** Cancel now really changes nothing: the new version is
  unpacked to one side and only swapped in once it is complete.
- **Picking a suggestion wrote it twice** in "What are you looking for?".
- **"At least" and "Stop after" were out of line** on the New search page.

### New

- **Smaller download:** 32 MB instead of 41 MB.
- **Lead details:** double-click a lead to see everything about that business in one
  window, with clickable links and a "Copy all" button.
- **Delete leads:** select one or more leads and press Delete (or right-click, or the
  "Delete selected" button).
- **The Leads page opens on your latest search.**
- **Past searches > Load** puts a search's leads on the Leads page and tells you it's ready.
- **Details to include:** on New search, untick the details you don't need (phone, social
  media, address...). They are left out of the Leads table and the Excel file.
- **Cities start unticked:** ticking a country lists its cities, and you tick the ones you want.

### Download and install

1. Download **GeoHarvest-v0.1.3-beta-windows.zip** below (Windows 10 and 11).
2. Right-click the zip and choose **Extract All**. Setup can't run from inside the zip.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click **GeoHarvest-Setup.exe**, then click **Install**.

**"Windows protected your PC"?** GeoHarvest is not code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.

**Already have GeoHarvest?** Use **Update now** in the app. If GeoHarvest won't open after
a Setup that didn't finish, install 0.1.3 by hand as above. Your searches, leads, settings
and AI key are kept either way.
