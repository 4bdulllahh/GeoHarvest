## GeoHarvest 0.2.1 beta: search around a place, more filters, owner names and CRM exports

### New

- **Search around a place:** on New search, choose **Around a place**, type an address or
  area ("Dubai Marina", "5th Avenue, New York") and a distance. GeoHarvest finds every match
  inside that circle. Countries and cities still work as before.
- **More filters:**
  - Google rating **at most** and reviews **at most**. A business with few reviews often
    needs more customers.
  - **Website: Any / Has a website / No website.** "No website" finds businesses you could
    build a site for.
  - **Opening hours:** open now, open 24 hours, open on weekends, or open on a day and time
    you pick. Each uses the business's own time zone.
- **Owner's name (optional):** GeoHarvest looks for the owner or founder on the business's
  website and in its domain record, and adds a LinkedIn search link so you can check. Many
  small businesses don't publish an owner's name, so expect it for some leads, not all.
- **Columns:** on the Leads page, **Columns...** switches columns on and off after a search.
  Export saves only the columns that are on.
- **More exports:** CSV files ready to import into **HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM and
  Salesforce**, and **Copy for Google Sheets**: it copies your leads and opens a new sheet, then
  you press Ctrl+V.
- **Proxies (optional):** in Settings, paste HTTP or SOCKS5 proxies, one per line, and test
  them. GeoHarvest takes turns between them for Google Maps searches and skips any that stop
  working. They are kept encrypted on your computer.
- **Switches everywhere:** every tick box is now an on/off switch.

### Download and install

1. Download **GeoHarvest-v0.2.1-beta-windows.zip** below (Windows 10 and 11).
2. Right-click the zip and choose **Extract All**. Setup can't run from inside the zip.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click **GeoHarvest-Setup.exe**, then click **Install**.

**"Windows protected your PC"?** GeoHarvest is not code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.

**Already have GeoHarvest?** Use **Update now** in the app. Your searches, leads, settings and
AI key are kept.
