## GeoHarvest 0.2.2 beta: the best leads, step by step

### New

- **Searches run in three clear steps:** first GeoHarvest searches Google Maps, then it visits
  the websites, then it picks the best leads. The progress bar follows the current step, and
  every number only goes up.
- **You get the best leads, shared fairly:** every lead gets a quality score (phone, website,
  email, rating, reviews, owner-claimed listing, real address, opening hours, social media),
  averaged with the AI score in Smart mode. "Stop after" keeps the best that many, taking turns
  between your cities and business types, so one big city can't take every place.
- **Leads that drop out are replaced:** if a website check rules a lead out (for example, it is
  too old for Startups), the next-best business already found takes its place.
- **When there aren't enough, GeoHarvest says why:** for example "Only 26 of the 40 leads you
  asked for: 44 older than 3 years, 14 no website...". Two buttons, **Add 10 more cities** and
  **Allow any company age**, carry the same search on.
- **Pick business types from a list:** click the box at the top of New search to open about
  140 business types in groups (Marketing, Health, Food, Property, Shops...). Typing narrows
  the list, a click adds or removes a type, and Enter adds one that isn't listed.
- **Clearer choices:** in Details to include and in Leads > Columns, a selected detail is
  highlighted and an unselected one is greyed out, instead of a switch beside each word.
- **LinkedIn from the business's website:** the company's LinkedIn page and the owner's own
  profile are taken from links on the business's website. "N/A" means the website doesn't link
  one. The old LinkedIn search links are gone.
- **New filter:** "Must show its address".

### Fixed

- **Fewer leads than asked for, with no reason given:** the search now explains what was left
  out and why.
- **Leads count going up and down during a search:** leads now appear once they are picked.
- **Leads with no address, city or country:** some businesses hide their address on Google.
  These now show "Service area" and take the city of the search when they are nearby. The ones
  far away (some were on another continent) are left out.
- **Startups and Established leaving out many businesses:** some countries hide when a website
  was registered. Those businesses are now kept, marked "Unknown", and ranked after the rest.

### Download and install

1. Download **GeoHarvest-v0.2.2-beta-windows.zip** below (Windows 10 and 11).
2. Right-click the zip and choose **Extract All**. Setup can't run from inside the zip.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click **GeoHarvest-Setup.exe**, then click **Install**.

**"Windows protected your PC"?** GeoHarvest is not code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.

**Already have GeoHarvest?** Use **Update now** in the app. Your searches, leads, settings and
AI key are kept.
