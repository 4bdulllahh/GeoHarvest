GeoHarvest: find business leads on Google Maps

HOW TO INSTALL

1. Extract this zip first: right-click it and choose "Extract All".
   (Setup needs the "runtime" folder next to it, so it can't run from inside the zip.)
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click GeoHarvest-Setup.exe.
3. Click Install. At the end, choose your shortcuts and whether GeoHarvest
   opens when Windows starts.

"Windows protected your PC"? GeoHarvest is not code-signed yet. Click
"More info", then "Run anyway".

"Smart App Control blocked an app"? Some Windows 11 PCs block every program
that isn't code-signed, with no "Run anyway". GeoHarvest can't be installed on
those PCs yet; a backup installer for them is coming in v0.3.1.

No administrator password needed. No account, no sign-in: your searches, leads
and settings stay on this computer, in %APPDATA%\GeoHarvest.

To remove GeoHarvest: Settings > Uninstall GeoHarvest inside the app, or
Windows Settings > Apps.
